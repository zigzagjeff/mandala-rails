### 2. EU Trademark Law (EUTMR Article 7 — Absolute Grounds for Refusal)

The European Union Trade Mark Regulation **explicitly prohibits** registration of:

| Category | Definition | Registrable? |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Generic terms** | Common name for goods/services | ❌ No |
| **Descriptive terms** | Describes characteristics (kind, quality, purpose) | ❌ No (unless acquired distinctiveness) |
| **Distinctive terms** | Arbitrary/fanciful | ✅ Yes |

**Relevance:**
- **"mandala"** = generic term for a spiritual/artistic symbol pattern → **Cannot be trademarked** for chart tools
- **"mandala chart"** = descriptive phrase for a chart using mandala structure → **Cannot be trademarked** without acquired distinctiveness
- **"MandalaChart"** (one word, camel-case) = potentially distinctive as a brand → **May be registrable**
